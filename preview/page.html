Start

  1. Press PLAY. Hold it for a second instead to fade the loops in; hold it again to fade out and stop.
  2. Play any key over the soundtrack: all 27 are notes.
  3. Press SCL for a new world, ARP to let it drift.

Hold SAVE for shortcuts

+ SCL
undo
+ PLAY
every loop back to step 1
+ REC
clear the selected track
+ K1–K4
haze, glint, drone, pulse levels

A tap of SAVE on its own saves the song. Every knob names what it changed on the screen's bottom line.

The screen

hazeglintdronepulsekeys

Each track is a lane of 16 steps; the lit column is where it is playing. The last lane is the keyboard. SELECT picks a track and PRESETS changes its sound; ALGORITHM picks the pad you play. On the main screen K1 is the keys volume and K2 the loops volume; MASTER sets both.

Buttons

SEQ
steps of the track: whites are steps, blacks mute, new loop, clear, remix, undo, shift
HOME
world: saved songs on whites, shuffle, undo, wipe, mood, space on blacks
EDIT
a track's sound and loop. With SELECT on KEYS: the WAVE page, drawn on screen (pad, wave, detune, chord)
ENV · LFO
shape · tape and tone. On KEYS: the ENVELOPE graph (attack, release, hold, volume) · TONE (tone, vibrato, space, scale)
FX · GLO
space sends: K1–K4 haze, glint, drone, pulse; PRESETS keys · mix
REC
keys write into the selected track

Computer keyboard

Play like a piano: A S D … are white keys, W E T … the black ones. Space is PLAY, ← → turn SELECT. Drag or scroll a knob, or tap its left or right half.
